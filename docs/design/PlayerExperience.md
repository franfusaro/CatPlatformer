# Player Experience

> **Purpose:** The intended moment-to-moment feel and emotional targets — what the player should *feel*, distinct from what the systems *do*.

## Feel targets (seed)

Drawn from [Vision](Vision.md) — the fantasy of two opposite-souled cats:

- **Default state = flow.** Fast, expressive, uninterrupted movement. The player
  should feel *fluent*, not careful, most of the time.
- **Swapping should feel like a single thought**, not a menu action — an instant
  control-flip (see [CoreLoop](CoreLoop.md)). Underneath, it's **shared leadership**,
  not character replacement — full principles: [CoreLoop — Swap
  Philosophy](CoreLoop.md#swap-philosophy).
- **Tension is punctuation.** Enclosed puzzle/boss rooms are the deliberate
  pressure spikes; the release back into flow is the reward.
- **Readability under speed.** The player must know *at a glance* which cat they
  control — the whole art direction serves this (see [narrative/Characters](narrative/Characters.md)).
- **Character before dialogue.** The opening minutes should establish who these two
  cats are — and that they're bonded — through idle behavior, environment
  interaction, and shared rituals, with minimal or no dialogue. See
  [CoreFantasy](CoreFantasy.md#behavioral-storytelling).
- **Progressive mastery.** A beginner→expert arc where the player stops thinking of
  Orange and Tuxedo as two separate characters and experiences the swap as one
  continuous movement system. See [gameplay/Movement — Mastery
  curve](gameplay/Movement.md#mastery-curve).
