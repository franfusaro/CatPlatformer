No main plot is written yet. Seed hooks that exist in the design:

- The pair can be **separated** (solo-cat sections) — a built-in "separation → reunion" beat (see [../levels/Tutorial](../levels/Tutorial.md)).
- Progression is relationship-driven (abilities *learnt from others* — see [../gameplay/Progression](../gameplay/Progression.md)), which implies a world of NPCs with their own stakes.

## Shared history

The backstory of how Orange and Tuxedo became bonded is settled (see
[Characters](Characters.md#shared-history) for the full beats): Tuxedo lived alone,
Orange arrived as a young kitten, protection grew into companionship, and Orange
grew up never knowing life without Tuxedo. This exists to *reinforce* their
established personalities (Tuxedo's protectiveness, Orange's adventurousness), not
to explain them away or replace them.

### Delivery: playable memories

The preferred direction for revealing this history is **short, optional playable
memories** rather than cinematic flashbacks — small interactive vignettes (kitten
Orange following Tuxedo everywhere, learning everyday cat behaviors together,
sharing meals/naps, discovering favorite places, forming the rituals that persist
into the present — see [Characters](Characters.md#shared-relationship--rituals)).
The intent is to let the player *experience* warmth, humor, and affection first-hand
rather than be told about it through exposition, and to lean on positive shared
memories rather than tragedy to build emotional investment.

This is a **content system**, not yet a committed feature — tracked unscoped in
[Ideas](../../Ideas.md#future-mechanics) pending the open questions below. This is
Wave 3+ (content) territory; it does not affect current Wave 0 scope
(see [Roadmap](../../production/Roadmap.md)).

### Open questions (not yet decided — do not assume answers)

- How are memories discovered within the world? (Cross-check against the **Found**
  ability source in [gameplay/Progression](../gameplay/Progression.md) once decided —
  may or may not reuse the same discovery pattern.)
- Are they entirely optional, or do a small number support the main narrative?
- Does completing a memory unlock a gameplay reward, a cosmetic
  (see [gameplay/Collectibles](../gameplay/Collectibles.md)), or purely emotional
  context?
- How does the final memory connect to the ending of the game?

### Risks to watch

- Overusing memories and disrupting the pacing of the main adventure (conflicts with
  Pillar 1, Flow first — see [DesignPillars](../DesignPillars.md)).
- Leaning too heavily on sadness instead of letting players build affection through
  joyful experiences — the whole point of this direction is to avoid that trap.
- Making the shared history the *sole* explanation for each character's personality
  instead of letting them read as complete individuals in the present (see
  [CoreFantasy](../CoreFantasy.md) for the same risk stated generally).
