## Before the game begins

1. Tuxedo lives independently.
2. Orange enters his life as a very young kitten.
3. Tuxedo protects Orange out of a sense of responsibility.
4. That responsibility grows into genuine companionship; Orange brings new meaning
   and spontaneity to Tuxedo's life.
5. Orange grows up knowing Tuxedo as his one constant companion — the root of his
   trust in Tuxedo and his fear of prolonged separation.
