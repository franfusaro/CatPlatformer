## Combat & bosses

- **Regular combat** = "who can hurt this?" gating — trash mobs die to Orange's
  dash-pounce and stay in flow; **armored/heavy enemies** force a swap to Tuxedo (see
  [Movement — enemy gating](Movement.md)).
- **Boss loop** (the swap *is* the combat):
  ```
  Boss armored / invulnerable
     → ⬛⬜ Tuxedo: FLOP stuns / cracks armor  (opens a window)
     → ⟳ SWAP
     → 🟠 Orange: dash-combo DPS during the window
     → window closes → repeat, dodging the whole time
  ```
- **Boss arenas = puzzle rooms** (teleport off, both cats present, exit locked until
  the boss falls) — reuses the same tech as [puzzle rooms](../levels/LevelStructure.md).
- Boss variety spectrum: puzzle-leaning (weak point only wall-cling Orange can reach
  while Tuxedo holds a lever) ↔ action-leaning (pure stun→DPS dance). Roster and flavor
  in [narrative/Bosses](../narrative/Bosses.md).

## Combat abilities

- 🟠 **Orange:** dash-combo DPS, dash-through swipes, air combos.
- ⬛⬜ **Tuxedo:** **the Flop** (ground-pound AoE stun), body-slam, big slow swats.
